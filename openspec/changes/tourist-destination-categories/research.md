# Research: tourist-destination-categories

Status: done. Collected 2026-09-29 by `sdd-research` (web only). Pending user confirmation of the destination tree.

## Tourist regions

Six-region tourism-marketing model (corroborated by Cancillería and a press source; no stable primary page after the 2023 ministry restructuring).

| Region | Provinces | Confidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Buenos Aires | CABA + Provincia de Buenos Aires | High |
| Córdoba | Córdoba | High |
| Cuyo | Mendoza, San Juan, San Luis (+ La Rioja per one source) | Medium |
| Litoral | Corrientes, Entre Ríos, Misiones, Chaco, Formosa, Santa Fe | High |
| Norte | Salta, Jujuy, Tucumán, Catamarca, Santiago del Estero (+ La Rioja per one source) | Medium |
| Patagonia | Neuquén, Río Negro, Chubut, Santa Cruz, Tierra del Fuego, La Pampa | High |

Notes: CABA is never a standalone tourism region. La Pampa is placed in Patagonia. La Rioja (Norte vs Cuyo) is unresolved across sources.

## Gaps and risks

- No primary argentina.gob.ar page reachable; region set high confidence, La Rioja placement medium.
- Demand ranking from PreViaje/press sources, not raw INDEC EOH tables; portal taxonomies not cross-checked.
- English names follow standard practice, not an official bilingual source.
- Costa Atlántica grouped into 4 buckets deliberately; per-balneario split would add 30+ items.
